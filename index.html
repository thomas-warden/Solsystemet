A living astronomical atlas.

Three experimental worlds made to explore, not just observe. Drag or use the controls. Try something, watch what changes, and ask why.

How to use it

Earth — Turn the globe with sunlight held fixed. Follow a real city from day to night. Clouds move independently; their motion is illustrative.

Jupiter — Speed up simulated atmospheric circulation, approach the Great Red Spot and compare scales. The wind field is artistic, not measured.

Saturn — Travel over and under the rings and track particles moving at different orbital speeds. Ring structures are simplified.

Representation & sources

The Earth surface derives from NASA Blue Marble imagery; the other planetary materials are reconstructions informed by spacecraft observation. Orbital periods and planetary dimensions follow NASA/JPL fact sheets. This is a scientific visualisation, not a live feed of observed weather.

Performance & access

If GPU rendering is unavailable the experience uses pre-rendered planetary illustrations; controls and science explanations remain available. Respect reduced motion, or pause the animation using the main control.
