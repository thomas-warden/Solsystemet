Experience: tap the visible Sun to send an illustrative gust; follow the magnetic response into the auroral sky. Open Earth to reveal the liquid metal that sustains its magnetic field.

Reference: the user's current Earth/tides screenshots establish textured planetary surfaces, quiet cream typography and direct manipulation. The rejected Aurora screenshots establish what to remove: detached dashes, thin abstract loops and instrument-heavy controls. Use the existing NASA Earth maps at full quality. Detailed technical cutaway illustration is the reference for the interior: readable layers, exposed faces and annotations, not a flat pie chart.

Composition: luminous textured Sun at left, Earth and its magnetic environment at right. One obvious action next to the star. Close interior and polar views preserve Earth as the dominant object. Gold plasma, copper/orange interior, blue magnetic field and green aurora remain distinct.

Asset plan: original procedural Sun/photosphere/corona; original 3D Earth cutaway with concentric geological layers, textured cross-sections and moving outer-core flow tracers. These are explanatory illustrations, not observations. Existing credited Earth/cloud/night maps are retained. No new remote dependencies.

Interaction: send a gust, watch its arrival and an illustrative energy release, then inspect the aurora. Four direct story positions provide stationary exploration. Inside Earth reveals the outer core; selectable layer annotations explain solid mantle, liquid outer core and solid inner core. Never imply the core powers auroras or pressure alone controls activity.
