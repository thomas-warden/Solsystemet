Generated image originals are retained in the working project's `assets/*.png`; the portable runtime uses only WebP. The model generated each image from a written art brief; no third-party photograph was passed to the image tool. No third-party photograph is redistributed as an application asset. Tool use does not supply a third-party stock-photo license, and these images must remain identified as generated illustrations.

The turtle, vampire squid, and dumbo images were opened at full image size, then reviewed at intended desktop, phone, and detail crops. Their general silhouette and teaching features are visible; fine anatomical accuracy and exact natural colour remain limits. The source pages in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) establish the factual text independently of the illustrations.

The illustrative depth encounters are 20 m, 700 m, and 3,000 m. They are neither habitat limits nor guaranteed sightings, and the app does not imply all three animals live on one real geographic transect. Deep animals are shown with added expedition lighting. Sizes and depth spacing are not to scale.

The Earth reference was inspected as a quality reference. Its code, Earth maps, and third-party 3D libraries are not part of this ocean app. The shared principles are local assets, subject-led composition, a visible pause control, a stationary inspection path, and explicit rendering lifecycle behavior.
