This exhibit adds directional lighting, color-derived ocean reflectance, an illustrated
atmospheric rim, a cloud shell and approximate cloud shadows. Cloud motion, lighting,
Earth orientation and animation speeds are illustrative. Images describe different
historical composites and do not represent current weather or a synchronized observation.
