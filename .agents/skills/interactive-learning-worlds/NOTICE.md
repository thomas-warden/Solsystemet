# Provenance

This skill was developed in the Solsystemet project for its owner. Its workflow incorporates lessons from that application's educational activities, rendering, accessibility, testing, and publication.

The optional `assets/patterns/anchored-labels.js` is adapted from Solsystemet's `orbit-labels.js` at source revision `f8d8fc182f73c62cb5314a7a5aff04020f4212f7`, with lifecycle cleanup added for reuse. The starter illustrations are simple authored geometric demonstrations, not scientific imagery or researched subject content.

The package does not include Solsystemet's planetary maps, third-party fonts, Three.js distribution, or other external media. Suggested source institutions and example learning briefs are research directions; they do not represent completed source verification for a new application.

This package does not change the license of the containing repository or assign a new license to the owner's code.
